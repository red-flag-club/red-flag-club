<p align="center"><b>🚩 Red Flag Club</b><br><i>The investment club that tells you when to walk away.</i></p>

**Red Flag Club** is a free, community-run project against pump-and-dump scams in small-cap stocks, the kind run through "free" WhatsApp and Telegram investing groups with a friendly "professor".

- **What:** a website with daily risk lists of penny stocks that are running up, a ticker look-up with the red flags behind each verdict, true stories from people who lost money (anonymised), a quiz that trains you to spot the script, and step-by-step help if it already happened to you.
- **Why:** these schemes take people's savings by building trust for months and then pushing one tiny stock "together". Warnings that arrive *before* the push measurably reduce how many people buy; warnings that arrive after don't. People who were hurt often stay silent out of shame, so the next group finds new victims.
- **How:** open-source checks that use public data (prices, SEC filing history), a scan of the whole market every trading day, and a review process where every change is specified, reviewed and tested before it ships. We never tell anyone to buy, and we never call a company a fraud; we show the red flags and the evidence.

👉 **Site:** https://red-flag-club.github.io · **Code:** [red-flag-club/red-flag-club](https://github.com/red-flag-club/red-flag-club) · **Stories:** [red-flag-club/stories](https://github.com/red-flag-club/stories)

**Lost money, or worried you're in a group like this right now?** Start with [what to do now](https://red-flag-club.github.io/#/help). Reporting to the SEC (sec.gov/tcr) and the FBI (ic3.gov) is free, and still worth doing years later. Anyone who asks for a fee to "recover" your money is very likely running the next scam.

*Not financial advice. Built by volunteers, for the community, by the community.*
