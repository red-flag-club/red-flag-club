# Security and privacy

Red Flag Club handles sensitive things: stories from people who lost money, and checks that people trust before they trade. Please report problems **privately**.

**How:** open a private advisory at [red-flag-club/red-flag-club → Security → Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/red-flag-club/red-flag-club/security/advisories/new). Don't open a public issue.

**Especially:**
- anything that could reveal a story author's identity or decrypt a submission;
- personal data, account numbers or brokerage data in any public repo or on the site;
- a way to make the site or the checks show a "buy" signal, or flip a verdict without the data changing;
- leaked tokens or keys.

**What happens next:** we aim to reply within 7 days, fix critical issues first, and credit you if you want.

**Not in scope:** reports that a specific stock is a scam. Use the "wrong verdict" or "red-flag report" issue forms, or report it to the SEC (sec.gov/tcr).
