# Getting help

**If you lost money to a stock "group", or think you're in one right now:**

1. **Stop sending money**, including any "fee", "tax", "deposit" or "compensation trade" to get earlier losses back. That is the second scam.
2. **Save everything:** chat exports, screenshots, brokerage confirmations, phone numbers, group names.
3. **Report it.** It's free, and it's still worth doing years later:
   - FBI Internet Crime Complaint Center: https://www.ic3.gov
   - SEC tips and complaints: https://www.sec.gov/tcr
   - FINRA Securities Helpline for Seniors: 844-574-3577
   - Your broker's fraud team
4. **Talk to someone you trust.** Shame keeps victims silent; you are not alone.

Step-by-step help: https://red-flag-club.github.io/#/help

**Questions about the project:** [Discussions](https://github.com/red-flag-club/red-flag-club/discussions). We can't give individual investment advice, and we never tell anyone what to buy.
