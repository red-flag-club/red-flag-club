# Code of Conduct

Red Flag Club adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), with the additions below, because many people here have just lost money they can't afford to lose.

## Our pledge

We make participation in this community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation, **and regardless of how much money someone lost or how they lost it.**

## Standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:
- Treating people who were scammed with respect. Nobody here is "stupid" or "greedy": these schemes are built by professionals to fool careful people.
- Giving and accepting feedback on code and specs with kindness and evidence.
- Owning our mistakes and learning from them.

Not acceptable:
- Blaming or mocking victims.
- **Posting anyone's personal data**: names, phone numbers, addresses, account numbers, chat screenshots, group links. This includes "naming and shaming" a suspected promoter. Report patterns, not people.
- Promoting any stock, group, signal service, "recovery" service or paid course.
- Calling a specific company or person a fraud as a statement of fact. Describe what happened and link public evidence (court filings, regulator actions).
- Harassment, sexualised language, trolling, insults, personal or political attacks.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may hide, edit or remove comments, issues and PRs that break this code, and may block people temporarily or permanently. Personal data is removed first and discussed afterwards.

Report a problem privately using GitHub's **Report content** menu on the comment, or open a private security advisory in [red-flag-club/red-flag-club](https://github.com/red-flag-club/red-flag-club/security/advisories/new) and mark it "conduct". Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers follow the Covenant's [enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
